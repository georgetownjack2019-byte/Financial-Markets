## 1. The one-line read

Six of these seven positions are the same trade wearing different clothes: **long duration, long the
AI-capex cycle**. The 10-year sits at 4.79% and the 30-year at 5.27% — multi-year highs — and the
16 September FOMC carries a *hike* as the consensus estimate (3.75% → 4.00%). **Circle is the only
name in the book that gets paid when that happens.**

The near-term tape is therefore not a stock-picking problem. It is one macro question — does the
Warsh Fed hike into a cracking labour market — asked seven times.

### CRCL — $88.76 · the natural hedge in this book

**Micro.** USDC circulation **$73.3bn** (+19% y/y); on-chain volume $14.8tn (+151% y/y); 63% of all
stablecoin transaction volume on 28% of supply. Q2: revenue $701m (+7%), adj. EBITDA $143m (+8%),
EPS $0.18 vs $0.16 est. **Reserve return fell 66bp y/y to 3.48%**, costing roughly $130m of income
that higher volumes would otherwise have produced. Balance sheet: $1.73bn cash, **zero debt**.
OCC national trust bank charter approved. Chelsea FC partnership announced 28 Aug.

**Macro transmission — the asymmetry.** Circle's revenue *is* short-rate income on reserves. A 25bp
hike on $73.3bn is roughly **+$183m of gross annualised reserve income**. A large share is shared
with distribution partners (historically well over half), so net capture is materially smaller and is
the number to watch — but the sign is the opposite of every other holding here. **If the Fed hikes on
16 September, CRCL is the position that benefits.**

**The hard date.** Circle launches its **Arc L1 mainnet on 16 September** — the same day as the FOMC —
with BlackRock, DTCC, Visa, Mastercard, ICE, Standard Chartered and MoneyGram named as founding
validators. That moves the story from issuance economics to transaction and enterprise fees, i.e.
from a rate proxy to a network. It is the highest-quality single catalyst on this whole list.

**The threat.** Major banks are moving from experimentation to competing directly for stablecoin
issuance (2 Sep coverage). GENIUS Act final rules land in **November**, enforcement in **January 2027**.
Rules that permit stablecoin rewards would compress Circle's spread; rules that entrench licensed
issuers would widen its moat.
